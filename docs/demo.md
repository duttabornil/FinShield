# FinShield: 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **FinShield**: AI-Powered Financial Fraud Defense Network  
> *Tagline: “Detect. Understand. Expose.”*

---

### Timing Breakdown
- **0:00 – 0:30**: Introduction & Dashboard Telemetry
- **0:30 – 1:15**: Trigger Suspicious Transaction & Forensic Investigation
- **1:15 – 2:00**: Fraud Network Graph & Mule Chain Inspection
- **2:00 – 2:45**: Coordinated Fraud Ring Injection & AI Explanation
- **2:45 – 3:00**: Defense Interventions & Wrap-up

---

### Step 1: Ecosystem Dashboard (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Action**: Open `http://localhost:5173`.
- **Speaker**:
  > *"Judges, modern financial fraud doesn't look like an amateur brute-force attack. It operates as distributed networks of mule accounts, account takeovers, and rapid multi-hop layering that slip past traditional rule engines.*
  >
  > *Meet **FINSHIELD**—an autonomous fraud defense network designed to **Detect, Understand, and Expose** illicit money movement in real time.*
  >
  > *On our dashboard, we see a live telemetry stream: 55+ transactions, 4 flagged high-risk anomalies, ₹180,000+ in suspicious money flow, and an active graph engine tracking connected fraud rings. Note that all data shown is 100% synthetic for this hackathon prototype."*

---

### Step 2: Open Attack Simulator (0:30 - 0:45)
- **Action**: Click **Simulator** in the sidebar (or the *"Launch Simulator"* button in the top navbar).
- **Speaker**:
  > *"To test our autonomous defense posture, let's open the Attack Vector Simulator."*

---

### Step 3: Trigger Suspicious Transaction (0:45 - 1:00)
- **Action**: Click **"Trigger Scenario"** on the **Suspicious Transaction** card.
- **Speaker**:
  > *"Let's simulate an unauthorized capital flight attempt. We trigger a sudden transfer of ₹48,000 from Ananya Sharma to an unverified beneficiary, Rohan Verma."*

---

### Step 4: Show 92/100 Risk & Forensic Breakdown (1:00 - 1:25)
- **Action**: Click **"Investigate Transaction"** in the glowing banner (or click the transaction row in the audit table).
- **Speaker**:
  > *"FinShield immediately intercepts the transfer, scoring it at **92/100 (CRITICAL)**.*
  >
  > *Look at the side-by-side behavioral comparison: Ananya's 30-day average transfer is ₹5,800. This ₹48,000 request is **8.3x higher than normal**. The beneficiary is brand new (+20 pts), the device is an unrecognized iPhone fingerprint (+15 pts), it occurred at 2:30 AM (+10 pts), and the recipient is linked to a flagged mule network (+30 pts).*
  >
  > *Notice our AI Forensic Explanation: generated deterministically without requiring any third-party API key, giving analysts an immediate plain-English audit trail. We have real-time defense actions right here: **Pause Transaction**, **Step-Up Biometric Verify**, or **Freeze Account**."*

---

### Step 5: Investigate Network Graph (1:25 - 1:45)
- **Action**: Click **"Inspect Graph"** (or select **Fraud Network** from the sidebar).
- **Speaker**:
  > *"Individual transactions only tell half the story. Fraud happens in networks. Let's switch to FinShield's interactive Cytoscape graph."*

---

### Step 6: Show Mule-Account Chain (1:45 - 2:05)
- **Action**: Click **"Chain #1 (5 hops)"** on the top Detected Mule Chains bar.
- **Speaker**:
  > *"Our graph engine's DFS cycle detector automatically illuminates the full money laundering conduit:*
  >
  > *$\text{Victim (Ananya)} \longrightarrow \text{Mule A (Rohan)} \longrightarrow \text{Mule B (Vikram)} \longrightarrow \text{Mule C (Amit)} \longrightarrow \text{Apex Crypto Hub}$*
  >
  > *Clicking on any node opens our Account Inspector, displaying live balance, total inflows, outgoing drain, and connected risk contagion."*

---

### Step 7: Trigger Coordinated Fraud Ring (2:05 - 2:35)
- **Action**: Go back to Simulator, click **"Trigger Scenario"** on **Coordinated Fraud Ring**.
- **Speaker**:
  > *"Now, let's escalate. What happens when an organized syndicate launches a coordinated attack? We trigger the Coordinated Fraud Ring.*
  >
  > *Watch the system response: FinShield sounds the alarm: **'COORDINATED FRAUD PATTERN DETECTED'**. New transactions are dynamically injected, our risk model escalates threat levels across all connected nodes, and the network graph expands in real time."*

---

### Step 8: Alerts & Interventions (2:35 - 3:00)
- **Action**: Click **Alerts** tab.
- **Speaker**:
  > *"In our Incident Threat Queue, high-priority alerts are prioritized with automated defense playbooks. With a single click, compliance teams can freeze the entire laundering cascade before capital exits to unrecoverable crypto desks or ATM switches.*
  >
  > *That is FinShield: **Detecting** anomalies, **Understanding** behavioral baselines, and **Exposing** hidden syndicate graphs.*
  >
  > *Thank you—we welcome your questions!"*
